01

Perpetual Inventory Audits

We provide systematic inventory audits to ensure accurate stock records, effective controls, and timely identification of discrepancies across inventory operations.

02

Business and Share Valuations

We provide independent and comprehensive business and share valuation services in accordance with the Companies Act, FEMA, and Income Tax Act. Our expertise also extends to startup and emerging business valuations, supporting fundraising, transactions, regulatory compliance, restructuring, and other strategic business requirements.

03

Social Impact Assessor

We provide Social Impact Assessment and social audit services aligned with applicable requirements under the Companies Act and CSR framework. Our expertise also supports organisations and Social Enterprises in measuring, evaluating, and reporting social impact for CSR initiatives and Social Stock Exchange-related requirements, strengthening transparency, accountability, and stakeholder confidence.

04

Stock Valuations

We provide professional stock valuation services to determine the fair and accurate value of inventories in accordance with applicable accounting standards and statutory requirements. Our valuations support financial reporting, audits, banking requirements, insurance, regulatory compliance, and informed business decision-making.

05

Consultation on Various Matters

We provide professional consultation on a broad range of business, financial, and regulatory matters under applicable provisions of the Companies Act, FEMA, Income Tax Act, and the Insolvency and Bankruptcy Code.

06

Costing Systems

We design, implement, and enhance costing systems and procedures to improve cost visibility, control, and informed decision-making.

07

Cost Study & Analysis

We conduct detailed cost studies to support accurate cost determination, effective cost control, and informed pricing decisions.
